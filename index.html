About me

I am an information engineer with a doctorate in theoretical condensed matter physics.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in photonics and electromagnetics at the Department of Information Engineering of the University of Padova.

I am mostly interested in describing wave phenomena in photonic and quantum systems. Some of my specific interests include:

  • Nonlinear wave propagation
  • Theoretical photonics and electromagnetism
  • Stochastic modeling for optical communications
  • Ultracold quantum gases
